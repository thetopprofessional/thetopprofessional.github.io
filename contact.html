Contact

Reach the editorial desk.

Questions about the publication, enquiries about being considered for a profile, and corrections to something already published all arrive at the same desk. Say which of the three you are writing about and it reaches the right editor faster.

01

Write to the desk

The desk is not yet taking messages.

No published address is in service, so there is no form here that would reach anybody. The routes below still work.

02

Routes

Three kinds of enquiry.

  • Editorial

    Questions about the publication, how a profile was prepared, or the sourcing behind a particular statement.

    Read how profiles are prepared

  • Recognition

    Enquiries about being considered for a profile, and questions about what consideration involves.

    Request recognition

  • Corrections

    Something on a profile is wrong, out of date, or rests on a source that does not say what we say it says.

    How corrections work
03

What happens next

What you can expect.

  1. 01

    It reaches an editor

    Messages go to the editorial desk, not to a sales queue. A correction is read by somebody who can act on it.

  2. 02

    A correction goes to the source

    We check the claim against the source it was published on, and the date an editor read it. If the source does not say what we said, the profile changes.

  3. 03

    We say what we did

    A correction that changes a published statement is recorded. We do not quietly edit a page and leave no trace of what it used to say.

A publication you cannot write to is a leaflet.
