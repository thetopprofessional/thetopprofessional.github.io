About The Top Professional

A record of professional standing that can be checked, sentence by sentence.

There is no shortage of places that will publish a flattering paragraph about a professional. What is missing is one where a reader can tell, without leaving the page, where each claim came from and when somebody last checked it.

That is the whole of it. Every statement here is recorded against a dated source, and on a wide screen the source is printed in the margin beside the sentence it supports. The list at the foot of every profile is the same set of sources, numbered and dated, and each reference in the text points at one of them.

What that costs us

It makes profiles shorter. Plenty of true and interesting things about a person cannot be published here, because nobody has produced a source for them. A profile that looks thin usually means the evidence is thin, and we would rather show that than pad it.

What we are not

We are not a ranking. Nothing here is ordered by merit, and nothing about being listed implies that someone is better than anybody who is not. We are not an awarding body, and we do not certify, accredit or license anyone.

Who writes it

Profiles are written by editorial staff from recorded sources, and approved by somebody who did not write them. Subjects can supply information, correct it, and withdraw their consent, and they do not get to approve the wording. That distinction is the point of the thing.

Contact
