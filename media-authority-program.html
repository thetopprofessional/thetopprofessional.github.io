03

Two measures

More reach is not always more authority.

Media reach

How broadly a release can travel across the publishing ecosystem a program opens. It is a question of breadth, and it is what a placement count measures.

Media authority

How recognizable the destinations in that network are. It is a question of which mastheads a reader already knows, and a placement count does not measure it at all.

This is why the prices do not simply climb with volume. Influence costs more than Essential and documents fewer placements, because it is sold on the second measure rather than the first.

Questions

The things worth asking.

Including the awkward ones.

What exactly is this program?

A 12 month media publishing program. Your professional story is submitted through a publishing and distribution network, published to the destinations in that network, and documented in a report you receive. It is one of the services this publication offers, not the whole of it.

What does the 12 month program include?

Access to your selected publishing network for the full term, distribution of your releases through it, and the publishing documentation that records where they appeared.

Why is the program annual?

Because a media presence is cumulative. One announcement is an event on one date. A year of publishing builds a footprint a reader can find, which is the thing the program is actually for.

Are specific publications guaranteed?

No. The networks include the destinations named on this page, and where a release appears depends on eligibility, network availability and publishing conditions at the time. The sample reports show what the networks have documented, not a promise about your release.

Is this editorial coverage?

No, and the distinction matters. This is press release publishing and distribution. A journalist choosing to write about you independently is a different thing, and no program can sell it.

What does publishing footprint mean?

The destinations recorded in the publishing report: one row for each published page, with the outlet and the address it appeared at.

Why is Influence more expensive than Essential when it shows fewer placements?

Because the two are not sold on the same measure. Essential is sold on breadth. Influence is sold on reaching destinations a reader recognises by name, and its sample documents a single release rather than a year, so its eight rows are not comparable with Essential’s 452.

Why would I choose Premium Plus?

Because it is the only level that is strongest on both measures. Its sample documents 798 placements across 728 domains, 88% of them on a publisher’s own domain, and it includes the recognizable destinations as well.

Can I buy a single month?

No. All four are 12 month programs, quoted monthly and committed annually. The monthly figure is how the annual investment is expressed, not a rolling subscription you can stop after one month.

Can you guarantee traffic, leads, sales or rankings?

No. The program delivers publishing, distribution and documentation. Anyone selling you guaranteed traffic or guaranteed search rankings from a press release is selling something they cannot control.

Why are some destinations listed with a different host?

Because that is where the page actually sits. Several recognizable mastheads publish their business and markets sections through FinancialContent or MarketMinute, so the published page carries the masthead but lives on the platform’s domain. Both facts are on this page so you are not surprised by the address bar.

What happens after I choose a program?

You reach the desk and the program is confirmed with you before anything is published. From there the sequence is the one set out above: your story is prepared, submitted through the network your program opens, and documented in the report you receive. Nothing is distributed in your name until you have agreed what it says.

Which program should I choose?

Basic to establish a footprint. Essential for the best balance of reach and investment. Influence when recognizable authority matters more than volume. Premium Plus when you want both.
