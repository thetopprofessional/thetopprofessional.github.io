Policy

If something here is wrong, we would rather fix it than argue about it.

How to get something changed, what happens when you ask, and the two things we will not do whoever is asking.

01

What to send

The profile, the sentence, and what is wrong with it.

If you can point at a source that shows the correct position, send that too, with a link or a document. A correction backed by a source is usually resolved the same week.

  1. 1

    Name the profile

    Which page, and which sentence on it.

  2. 2

    Say what is wrong

    The correct position, stated plainly.

  3. 3

    Point at a source

    A link or a document showing it. This is what makes a correction quick rather than slow.

02

What happens

We go back to the source the sentence cites.

The published sentence is compared with the source it was built from. One of three things follows.

  • The source was misread

    The entry is corrected and the profile is rebuilt from it.

  • The source itself was wrong

    If a better one exists, the old source is superseded and the new one is recorded with its own date.

  • Two credible sources disagree

    The claim is marked disputed. A disputed claim is removed from the published page and stays off it.

03

Limits

What we will not do.

A subject can withdraw consent at any time, and their profile comes down in full. What they cannot do is keep the page and edit the parts of it they dislike.

  • Remove an accurate, sourced statement

    We do not take down a statement that is true and cited because a subject would prefer it were not there.

  • Add a claim because someone wants it

    We do not add a claim because someone would like it to be true. It has to come from a source first, like everything else.

04

The record

Changes are logged, and we will tell you.

Every change to a source, a claim, a draft or a published page is recorded with who made it and when. If you want to know whether a profile has been altered since you read it, ask.
