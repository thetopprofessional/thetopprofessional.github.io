Policy

Corrections

How to get something changed, and what happens when you ask.

If something here is wrong, we want to fix it, and we would rather fix it quickly than argue about it.

What to send

Tell us the profile, the sentence, and what is wrong with it. If you can point at a source that shows the correct position, send that too, with a link or a document. A correction backed by a source is usually resolved the same week.

What we do with it

We go back to the source the sentence cites and compare it with what was published. One of three things follows. If the source was misread, the entry is corrected and the profile is rebuilt from it. If the source itself was wrong and a better one exists, the old source is superseded and the new one is recorded with its own date. If two credible sources disagree, the claim is marked disputed, and a disputed claim is removed from the published page and stays off it.

What we will not do

We do not remove an accurate, sourced statement because a subject would prefer it were not there, and we do not add a claim because someone would like it to be true. A subject can withdraw consent at any time, and their profile comes down in full. What they cannot do is keep the page and edit the parts of it they dislike.

Changes are logged

Every change to a source, a claim, a draft or a published page is recorded with who made it and when. If you want to know whether a profile has been altered since you read it, ask.

How to reach us

An editorial address has not been published yet. Until it is, the contact form on an individual profile reaches that person directly, and they can pass a correction to us.

The Top Professional

Every statement on this site is recorded against a dated source. Corrections are reviewed against that source.
